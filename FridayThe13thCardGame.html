Rules of the night

🃏Mother's head is found

The first joker is Mrs. Voorhees. The player who finds her revives one of their own fallen counselors, or must revive another player if all their own counselors are alive.

☠️Aces & twos

An ace or a 2 kills one of the active player’s living counselors. If both counselors are dead, that player is out.

🔪Find Jason

The second joker reveals Jason and ends the game: the player who finds him wins, provided they are still alive.

🏚️Last one standing

If all other players are out before Jason is found, the last living player wins.

House-rule note: This digital version lets players draw from each cabin until a joker appears or its pile is empty, applies danger cards to the active player, and ends immediately when Jason is found.
