Friday the 13th Card Game

Was watching Jason lives, and they mentioned a Jason card game. I looked and looked but could not find any so made one instead. Played with friends and we had a blast. Sharing for Halloween time and the next Friday the 13th.

Friday the 13th Card game

Game can accommodate for up to 6 players

Setup:
- take out all face cards, but leave jokers in

- shuffle cards

- make 6 piles of 7 cards (each pile is a cabin)

- each player takes 2 or more face cards to represent counselors

Rules:

aces and 2's kill your counselor. if both counselors are dead you are out.

find Mrs. Voorhees' head & Jason before you are killed.

If you draw the 1st joker that is Mrs. Vorhees' head and you can revive one of your counselors.

If both of your counselors are still alive you must revive another player

whoever is the last person alive or whoever finds the second joker killed Jason and is the winner.

Search the cabins, Find Mother head before Jason kills you. Then find and kill Jason To win the Game.
